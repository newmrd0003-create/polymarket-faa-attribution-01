Where these prices come from

Each case file <id>.in holds, for every market of Polymarket's event "Next CEO of NVIDIA?" (slug next-ceo-of-nvidia,
https://polymarket.com/event/next-ceo-of-nvidia) that had traded by the case's checkpoint `at`, the market's Yes price
on a 1-minute UTC grid, for the 72 hours ending at `at` (or from the market's first minute, when it started later).

The prices are Polymarket's public price history, recorded by make.fun's recorder (collector/polymarket-forecast in
the make.fun repository): no key or account is needed to fetch them again.

  GET https://clob.polymarket.com/prices-history?market=<yes_token>&startTs=<unix s>&endTs=<unix s>&fidelity=1

answers {"history":[{"t":<unix seconds>,"p":<price>}, ...]}, about one point a minute, p the midpoint of the Yes
token's order book (a window is at most 15 days; Polymarket may stop serving 1-minute points for old windows). A
case's minute m holds the LAST point with t <= m (m the minute's first second), carried forward through minutes with
no point of their own; a market's grid starts at the first whole minute at or after its first point. Prices are as
Polymarket gives them, unrounded. The markets (each one's conditionId, name, slug and Yes token) are the event's
markets that were trading when the contest's universe was frozen, from
GET https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events/slug/next-ceo-of-nvidia.
